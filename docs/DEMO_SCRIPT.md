# Mlinzi — demo video script (~90 seconds)

Record the terminal running `./mlinzi demo-full`. Narrate over it — don't
wait for each block to finish before speaking; let the text scroll behind
your voice.

---

**[0:00 – 0:12] — Open on the terminal, before running anything**

> "In Kenya, if a police officer demands a bribe, most people don't know
> IPOA exists — let alone that it's free, toll-free, and works without
> mobile data. Mlinzi closes that gap. This is a live run, not a mockup."

**[0:12 – 0:15] — Type and run `./mlinzi demo-full`, let Step 1 print**

> "First: know. Every fact here is sourced and dated — including where two
> official sources disagree, which we show rather than hide."

**[0:15 – 0:35] — Let Step 2 print**

> "Second: report, anonymously. The moment this is filed, it's cryptographically
> chained. No name required — and no one, not even the institution
> receiving it, can quietly edit or delete it afterward."

**[0:35 – 0:55] — Let Step 3 print, through case creation**

> "Reporting can be dangerous. So if this is a safety report, the reporter
> sets a dead man's switch. Their evidence key is split across three
> guardians — a lawyer, a journalist, family. No single one of them, and not
> us either, can unlock it alone."

**[0:55 – 1:15] — Let the escalation and release print**

> "If they go silent, two guardians acting together — and only together —
> can release it. Silence stops being a way to make a report disappear."

**[1:15 – 1:25] — Let the final verification lines print**

> "And every step — the report, the check-ins, the release — is
> independently verifiable. Not a claim. A command anyone can run."

**[1:25 – 1:30] — Cut to black or the GitHub repo page**

> "Mlinzi. Know. Report. Be protected."

---

## Notes for recording

- Increase your terminal font size before recording — judges will watch this
  on a laptop screen, often at reduced video quality.
- If your terminal supports it, a subtle color scheme (dark background,
  light text) reads better on video than default white.
- Do one full unedited take first as a safety net, then a polished take.
- Total run time of `demo-full` is under a second — you are narrating over a
  paused/slowed playback or scrolling text, not real-time output. Consider
  piping through `less` or adding short `sleep` pauses between sections if
  you want the terminal itself to pace with your voice, or simply record
  yourself scrolling through the already-printed output while narrating.
